Tony Stark is the superhero known as Iron Man, a founding member of the Avengers, and one of Marvel's most influential heroes.

A brilliant engineer and inventor, Tony inherited Stark Industries from his father and became one of the world's leading technology innovators. Early in his career, the company manufactured advanced weapons for military use.

While demonstrating one of his weapons overseas, Tony was captured and seriously injured. Forced to build weapons for his captors, he instead created the first Iron Man armor and escaped. The experience transformed his outlook on life and inspired him to dedicate his technology to protecting others.

Tony continuously improves his suits of armor, developing advanced artificial intelligence systems, flight technology, energy weapons, and defensive capabilities. His inventions often push the boundaries of science and engineering.

Although Tony is known for his confidence, humor, and charisma, he struggles with guilt over the consequences of his past decisions. Much of his character development centers on accepting responsibility for his actions and learning what it truly means to be a hero.

Within the Marvel Cinematic Universe, Tony plays a central role in forming the Avengers, defeating global threats such as Loki and Ultron, mentoring Peter Parker, and ultimately sacrificing himself to stop Thanos during the events of Avengers: Endgame.

Tony Stark's legacy is defined not only by his technology but also by his willingness to put the safety of others above himself when it matters most.
