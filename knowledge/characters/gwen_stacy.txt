Gwen Stacy, also known as Spider-Woman or Ghost-Spider, is a Spider-Person from Earth-65.

In Gwen's universe, she—not Peter Parker—was bitten by the radioactive spider. Peter later transformed himself into the Lizard in an attempt to become powerful enough to fight alongside her. During their confrontation, Peter died, leaving Gwen burdened with guilt and causing the public to blame Spider-Woman for his death.

Because of these events, Gwen often isolates herself from others despite wanting genuine friendships. Music becomes an important emotional outlet, and she performs as the drummer in the band The Mary Janes.

Gwen is highly intelligent, athletic, and an experienced Spider-Person. Like other Spider heroes, she possesses enhanced strength, agility, Spider-Sense, and wall-crawling abilities.

Throughout the Spider-Verse films, Gwen develops a close friendship with Miles Morales. Their relationship is built on trust, shared experiences, and understanding the challenges of balancing superhero responsibilities with ordinary life.

In Across the Spider-Verse, Gwen initially follows Miguel O'Hara's rules and joins the Spider Society, believing that protecting the multiverse requires difficult sacrifices. However, after witnessing Miles' determination to save his father, she begins questioning those beliefs and ultimately chooses to stand beside her friends instead.

Gwen's story explores themes of guilt, forgiveness, belonging, and learning to trust others again after experiencing loss.
