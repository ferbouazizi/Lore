Miles Morales is one of the most well-known versions of Spider-Man in Marvel Comics and the central protagonist of the Spider-Verse films.

Miles lives in Brooklyn, New York, with his parents, Jefferson Davis and Rio Morales. Before becoming Spider-Man, he was an ordinary teenager attending Brooklyn Visions Academy while trying to meet the expectations of his family.

Miles gains spider-like abilities after being bitten by a genetically engineered spider that originated from another universe. His powers include enhanced strength, agility, wall-crawling, Spider-Sense, camouflage that allows him to become nearly invisible, and a bio-electric "Venom Blast" capable of stunning opponents.

Unlike Peter Parker, Miles begins his journey with very little confidence. Throughout the Spider-Verse films he gradually learns that becoming Spider-Man is not about replacing someone else but becoming the best version of himself.

Miles values compassion, optimism, and protecting the people he loves. His relationship with his parents, especially his father Jefferson and his mother Rio, plays a major role in shaping his decisions.

One of the defining themes of Miles' story is that anyone can wear the mask. He represents the idea that heroism comes from courage, empathy, and perseverance rather than experience or perfection.

In Across the Spider-Verse, Miles rejects the belief that his future has already been decided. He believes every person should have the freedom to choose their own path, even if that means challenging the rules established by the Spider Society.
