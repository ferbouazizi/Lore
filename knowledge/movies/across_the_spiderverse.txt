Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse is a 2023 animated superhero film produced by Sony Pictures Animation. It is the sequel to Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018) and the second installment in the Spider-Verse trilogy.

The story follows Miles Morales more than a year after becoming Spider-Man. As he struggles to balance his responsibilities as a superhero with school and family life, Gwen Stacy unexpectedly returns and introduces him to the Spider Society, an organization made up of Spider-People from countless parallel universes.

The Spider Society is led by Miguel O'Hara, also known as Spider-Man 2099. Their mission is to protect the stability of the multiverse by preserving "canon events"—key moments that every Spider-Person is expected to experience, such as the loss of a loved one or a police captain. Miguel believes preventing these events could threaten entire universes.

Miles disagrees with this philosophy after learning that his father, Jefferson Davis, is destined to become a police captain and die as part of his own canon event. Refusing to accept that fate, Miles decides to fight for the belief that anyone should be able to write their own future.

During the film, it is revealed that the spider which originally bit Miles came from another universe. Because of this, Miguel considers Miles an anomaly that was never supposed to become Spider-Man.

The movie explores themes of destiny, identity, responsibility, family, and free will. Rather than accepting that suffering is unavoidable, Miles challenges the idea that people are trapped by fate.

Across the Spider-Verse is known for its innovative animation style, with each universe featuring its own artistic direction and visual identity. The film ends on a cliffhanger, leading directly into the next installment, Spider-Man: Beyond the Spider-Verse.
