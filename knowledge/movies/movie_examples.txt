Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018) follows Miles Morales as he discovers his abilities and meets Spider-People from multiple universes. The film popularized the Spider-Verse concept and won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature.

Iron Man (2008), directed by Jon Favreau and starring Robert Downey Jr., introduced the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU). Its success established the foundation for one of the highest-grossing film franchises in history.

The Avengers (2012) was the first MCU crossover event that united multiple heroes in a single film. Directed by Joss Whedon, it demonstrated that interconnected storytelling could succeed on a blockbuster scale.

Avengers: Infinity War (2018) and Avengers: Endgame (2019) concluded the Infinity Saga, bringing together dozens of Marvel characters in one of the largest crossover events in cinema.
