The Avengers are a team of superheroes assembled to protect Earth from threats too dangerous for any individual hero.

The original comic team debuted in 1963 and was created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby.

Core members have included Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Hulk, Black Widow, Hawkeye, Scarlet Witch, Vision, Black Panther, Doctor Strange, Spider-Man, Captain Marvel, and many others.

Unlike organizations with permanent memberships, the Avengers roster changes over time depending on global threats and storylines.

The team has faced enemies including Loki, Ultron, Kang the Conqueror, and Thanos.

Although each member has unique abilities and personalities, the Avengers succeed through cooperation, leadership, and combining their strengths to solve problems no single hero could overcome alone.
