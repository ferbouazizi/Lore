Iron Man is the superhero identity of Tony Stark, a billionaire inventor, engineer, and industrialist.

Tony Stark inherits Stark Industries, one of the world's leading technology companies. During a weapons demonstration, he is captured and seriously injured. While imprisoned, he builds the first Iron Man suit to escape captivity.

Following this experience, Stark decides to stop manufacturing weapons and instead uses his technology to protect others.

Iron Man's suits are powered by an Arc Reactor and equipped with advanced systems including flight, repulsor blasts, missiles, artificial intelligence assistance, and modular armor upgrades.

Tony Stark is a founding member of the Avengers and plays a major role in defending Earth against threats including Loki, Ultron, and Thanos.

His character explores themes of innovation, responsibility, sacrifice, redemption, and the consequences of technological advancement.
