Spider-Man is a superhero created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko, first appearing in Amazing Fantasy #15 in 1962.

Peter Parker is the most recognizable Spider-Man. After being bitten by a radioactive spider, he develops enhanced strength, agility, reflexes, the ability to cling to walls, and a heightened danger sense known as "Spider-Sense."

Raised by Aunt May and Uncle Ben in Queens, New York, Peter initially uses his powers selfishly before Uncle Ben is murdered. That tragedy teaches him the lesson that defines his life:

"With great power comes great responsibility."

Peter constantly struggles to balance two lives. While protecting New York from supervillains, he also deals with university, work, friendships, finances, and maintaining relationships with the people he cares about.

Some of Spider-Man's closest allies include Aunt May, Mary Jane Watson, Gwen Stacy, Miles Morales, and members of the Avengers.

His most well-known enemies include Green Goblin, Doctor Octopus, Venom, Sandman, Electro, Mysterio, Vulture, Kraven the Hunter, and Kingpin.

Spider-Man is known for his intelligence, quick thinking, scientific talent, and sense of humor, often using jokes during combat to distract opponents.
